FAQ

Questions we've already heard

Doesn't Linktree already do this? +

No — Linktree points people to your Shopify, Etsy, or PayPal. It doesn't have any selling functionality. LiveCart is a live checkout page: buyers see your items, claim them, and pay on the spot — all in one place, in real time.

Do I need a Stripe account or ABN? +

You'll connect a bank account to receive payments — no separate Stripe account needed on your end. No ABN required for personal sellers.

Does this connect to my Instagram account? +

No — and that's intentional. No Instagram API means no risk of being locked out. You simply share your LiveCart link in your bio, story, or comments. It's just a URL — works anywhere.

What stops two people buying the same item? +

When a buyer taps Claim, the item is locked to them while they complete payment. If they don't pay within a few minutes, it releases back to available. Only one person can claim and pay for each item.

How much will it cost? +

We're finalising pricing, but it'll be a simple flat monthly fee — no commission on your sales. Early waitlist members get a meaningful discount and a say in what we build. Join the list to be first to know.
